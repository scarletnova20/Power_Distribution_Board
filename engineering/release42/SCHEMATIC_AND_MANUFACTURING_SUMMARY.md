# PDB schematic and manufacturing review

Reviewed revision: `engineering/release42/release_candidate.kicad_sch` and
matching PCB/project. Root-level projects are earlier checkpoints.
Outdated schematic annotations were corrected to 42 mm, 4S and external 9 V DJI.

## Status indicator

The circuit already contained D3 and R13, including routed PCB footprints.
This update identifies D3 as `GREEN 5V ON`, corrects its inherited Schottky-diode
description, and adds `5V ON` to the front silkscreen beside the LED near the
bottom edge at board coordinate (74, 228) mm. No duplicate LED was needed.

Connection: regulated `/out` -> R13 (1 kohm, 1%) -> D3 anode; D3 cathode -> PGND.
At 5 V with an assumed 2 V LED forward voltage, current is about 3 mA and resistor
loss about 9 mW. Exact LED ordering code and brightness remain assembly-BOM
selection items. The selected footprint is green 0805, resistor footprint 0603.
The LED indicates an energized regulator output, not a precision voltage window,
80 A load capability, correct BEC outputs, or a complete self-test. It may also
illuminate if this rail is powered externally. An unlit LED alone cannot
distinguish missing supply from regulator or LED-branch failure.

## Circuit summary

| Block | Actual implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Input | Custom 4S2P Li-ion system, nominal about 14.8 V, full 16.8 V. External battery harness/fuse feeds J1. F1 is external, not an onboard fuse holder. |
| Reverse polarity | U5 LM74502HDDFR drives four parallel ISC027N10NM6 N-channel MOSFETs Q1-Q4 with individual gate resistors. Their sources face the fused battery. This is reverse-polarity protection; body diodes permit startup current, so inrush limiting/load disconnect is not implemented. |
| Transient suppression | D2 SMCJ28CA is across BAT_FUSED and PGND upstream of the MOSFETs. Suitability for the 4S downstream equipment and clamp-current path remains unresolved. |
| Main power/current sensing | Two parallel 0.5 milliohm shunts R10/R11 give nominal 0.25 milliohm combined resistance. Protected, sensed battery power feeds J12 (ESC) and the external BEC inputs. At ideal 80 A sharing, shunt drop is 20 mV and combined loss 1.6 W. |
| Flight-controller supply | U1 LM5576 buck regulator, D1 freewheel diode, L1 33 uH inductor and input/output filtering provide nominal 5.019 V; intended FC/telemetry load up to 3 A. C9 is 120 uF 10SVPC120M. Compensation is modeled, not bench-qualified. |
| Digital monitoring | INA228 U3 measures shunt/bus signals and connects by SDA/SCL to J2. Address is configured as 0x45. U4 TLV75533 supplies its 3.3 V rail. |
| Analog monitoring | INA169 U2 provides the analog current test signal at TP2; a divider provides battery-voltage test signal at TP3. These test signals are separate from the J2 digital interface. |
| FC connector | J2 pins 1/2: regulated 5 V; 3: SCL; 4: SDA; 5/6: PGND. Harness mating orientation still needs final assembly verification. |
| External loads | J3/J4 accept external 5 V/9 V servo BEC connections; J5-J7 are 5 V servo headers and J8 is the 9 V servo header. J10/J11 handle external 9 V DJI BEC/output. BEC1 is the external Pi BEC interface; TP1 is a 5 V Pi test branch, not a qualified 5 A Pi delivery connector. These voltages are not all generated by the onboard buck. |
| Layout | 42 x 42 mm, four copper layers, presently 70 um each. Heavier copper is under evaluation, not implemented. |

## Manufacturing disposition: NOT READY

Fresh automatic results are recorded in `candidate_verification.json` and
`led_verification.json`. They establish checked connectivity and layout rules;
they do not close these remaining release gates:

1. The 80 A continuous rating is unqualified: positive/negative current paths,
   current sharing and a specified heatsink/thermal assembly need completion
   and prototype testing at 50 C ambient without guaranteed airflow.
2. The retained 42 mm heavier-copper process is not accepted. The 4 oz width
   screen found 900 existing segments below the reviewed process minimum.
   Three-ounce copper remains a process-review candidate. Minimum finished
   copper, via-barrel plating and filled/capped via-in-pad fabrication must be
   agreed and encoded, then routing checked against those actual rules.
3. Hot-plug/inrush, TVS/ESC/BEC transient and fuse/harness coordination remain
   open. Exact cells, BMS/fault current, wiring and BEC startup behavior are
   unknown and cannot be inferred from the pack C-rating.
4. Exact assembly BOM, including the LED ordering code and remaining passives,
   effective ceramic capacitance, connector/harness pinout and polarity need
   final review. Buck stability and sensing accuracy need bench validation.
5. Final manufacturing layers and assembly outputs must be generated and
   visually reviewed only after the accepted design/process changes. No
   fabrication release or production order has been made.

The current files are an engineering candidate, not a production-ready package.
Original pre-label files are preserved in `before_led_status/`.
